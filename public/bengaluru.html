About the Summit

The 29th edition of the Bengaluru Tech Summit (BTS 2026) took place from 17–19 November 2026 at the Bangalore International Exhibition Centre (BIEC) under the theme 'AI & Beyond'.

As one of Asia's premier technology gatherings, the summit showcased Karnataka and Bengaluru as global centres of innovation — bringing together industry leaders, researchers, startups, investors and policymakers from around the world.

While spotlighting the transformative power of artificial intelligence, BTS 2026 explored the broader frontier of emerging technologies — from deep tech and advanced digital ecosystems to breakthrough scientific and industrial innovation.

Event Location: Bangalore International Exhibition Centre (BIEC)
Date: 17–19 November 2026
Time: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
